# Week 18 — Final written examination

**Software Security 2 (02005094) · one 150-minute exam · date, time and room to be announced.** The KOSEN final exam period is 8–16 March 2027 (the calendar says dates may change). Your instructor will post the exact date, time, room and allowed aids in an official notice.

The 150 minutes are: 10 minutes to hand out papers and give instructions, 130 minutes to write and check your answers, and 10 minutes to collect papers. The exam has ten questions worth 10 marks each (100 marks): five Basic and five Technical. It counts **10%** of your course grade (5% Basic + 5% Technical).

**What it covers:** the whole term, mostly Weeks 10–15: API security, memory safety, supply-chain trust, cloud and container review, AI output and tool limits, and DevSecOps. You get marks for explaining what is wrong, how to fix it, which checks prove the fix, and what the fix still cannot prove.

**How to prepare:** do the [practice paper](exam.md). The real exam paper is different, but it covers the same ten topics in the same format. You receive it only on the day.
