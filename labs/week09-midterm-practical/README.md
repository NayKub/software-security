# Week 9 — Midterm individual practical (on paper)

**Software Security 2 (02005094) · individual work · Tuesday 29 December 2026, 09:05–11:50, in our usual classroom.** 165 minutes in total: 10 minutes of instructions, 145 minutes on the tasks (about 130 on the four stations and 15 to check), 10 minutes to collect papers.

**This is a paper exam.** You get printed code, scanner results and test output, and you write your answers on the paper. **Closed book: bring your student ID card and a pen (blue or black).** No computer, phone, notes or AI.

There are **four stations (A, B, C, D)**. Each station is worth 25 points, so the practical is out of 100. Those 100 points count as **12% of your course grade**. What each station asks and how it is marked: **[practical brief](ctf.md)**.

**What to revise:** Week 1 and Week 2 (threat models and scanner results), Week 3 (password hashing), Week 4 (SQL and command injection) and Week 6 (login tokens and "who owns this object" checks). Week 5 is not on the practical. The Week 7 practice checkpoints are the best warm-up: practise writing the fixes **by hand**, without running them.

**What to expect on the day:**

- You write code by hand. Small syntax slips do not lose marks if the idea is clear.
- You may be shown the test output from someone else's fix and asked what it means.
- You get marks for finding the problem, explaining it, writing your own fix, and writing one test that should work and one that should be refused, with the result you expect.
